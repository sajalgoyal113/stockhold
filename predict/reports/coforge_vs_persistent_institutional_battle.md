## 3. Why Persistent Fails the Valuation Gate (The "High P/E Trap")

$$\text{P/E Multiple Derating Penalty} = \frac{28.8\text{x (Exit Base P/E)}}{49.6\text{x (Current P/E)}} - 1 = -41.9\%$$
